[CRO-MUXON] Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-26

Cro-Muxon is a keyboard-driven window-tiling workspace manager for Chrome. This policy explains what data the extension touches and, more importantly, what it does not do with it.

Summary

Cro-Muxon does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data. Everything it stores stays on your device, in your own browser's local extension storage. The extension makes no network requests of any kind.

What Cro-Muxon stores, and where

All data is kept in Chrome's chrome.storage.local, which is private to your browser profile and is never synced to a server or sent anywhere:

  • Workspace layout — the tree of split panes you create, the window IDs belonging to each workspace, and the screen bounds of each pane, so the extension can restore and re-tile your layout.
  • Tab titles and URLs of windows you have chosen to manage — used only to show the pane picker (the on-screen list of your workspaces) and to restore a workspace's tabs when you save/load a layout to a local JSON file.
  • The ID of your last-focused window — used to highlight the correct pane when you open the picker.

None of this is readable by other extensions, other websites, or by us — it lives entirely inside your own browser installation.

Permissions Cro-Muxon requests, and why

  • tabs — to read tab titles/URLs for the windows you manage, and to open, switch, or navigate tabs when you use the pane picker.
  • storage — to save your workspace layout locally, as described above.
  • sidePanel — to host the extension's dashboard UI.
  • system.display — to read your screen's usable work area so panes can be tiled to fill it correctly (no display content is read, only geometry).
  • history / bookmarks — to power the :open command's autocomplete suggestions when you're typing a URL to navigate to. Searches are performed locally via Chrome's own history/bookmarks APIs; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • alarms — to keep the extension's background service worker alive during multi-step window operations (e.g. splitting a pane); this does not read or store any user data.
  • clipboardRead / clipboardWrite — to support copying a pane's URL and pasting it into another pane directly from the dashboard (the y / p shortcuts). Clipboard contents are only read at the moment you press p, are used solely to navigate the pane you're pasting into, and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Local export/import

If you use the Save/Load-to-disk feature, your workspace layout (pane structure, bounds, and tab URLs) is written to or read from a JSON file you explicitly choose on your own filesystem. This file is created and read only on your device — Cro-Muxon never uploads it anywhere.

Third parties

Cro-Muxon does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services, and does not communicate with any server operated by us or anyone else.
